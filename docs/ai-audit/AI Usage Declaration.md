## AI USAGE DECLARATION

Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI), particularly **ChatGPT by OpenAI**, was used as a supporting tool during the development and documentation of the _IoT-Based Fire Detection and Warning System_ project.

AI assistance was primarily used for exploring relevant scientific literature, understanding technical concepts, reviewing the ESP32-based system architecture, discussing sensor integration and fire detection logic, and improving the organization and academic language of the final report.

The project team critically reviewed AI-generated suggestions against the implemented source code, hardware design, technical documentation, and available functional testing results. AI-generated content was not automatically accepted as technically correct. In particular, the team distinguished between implemented features and proposed future improvements, including remote monitoring through IoT platforms such as Blynk.

**All final design decisions, implementation activities, experimental observations, and conclusions remain the responsibility of the project members.** AI was used to assist learning, research, and documentation rather than replace independent engineering judgment.

Selected AI-assisted decisions, verification activities, and reflections are documented separately in the **AI Audit Log**.
